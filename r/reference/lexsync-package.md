# lexsync: Lexical Optimisation and Hardware-Timed Experiment Generation

A cross-platform toolkit that unifies many-language lexical-corpus
access, parallel multidimensional stimulus matching, deterministic
pseudoword generation, counterbalancing and the automated generation of
experiments from a declarative trial-event model, for 'PsychoPy',
'OpenSesame' and the browser ('jsPsych'). The laboratory targets bind
electroencephalography onset triggers to the stimulus flip. It is the R
member of a dual-language pair; a structurally identical 'Python'
package is also provided. Several paradigms (factorial word contrasts,
lexical decision, priming, self-paced reading and cued categorisation)
are supported, and each design is accompanied by a machine- and
human-readable materials datasheet for reproducibility.
