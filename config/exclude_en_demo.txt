# Words the English demonstrations leave out of their candidates.
#
# Every English corpus and generated design in config/ names this file under
# exclude_words. Those designs also select only the forms corpora/derived/en_v2.csv
# keeps, which SUBTLEX-US lists and writes in lower case in at least a tenth of their
# uses. Some unsuitable forms pass that screen, and this list leaves them out. A form
# is listed when a demonstration selected it and, in its dominant use, it is
#
#   1. offensive, taboo or distressing: an obscenity, profanity, sexual slang or a
#      word for sexual violence; or
#   2. not a standard English word: an abbreviation, a word of another language, a
#      proper name, a form that is no word at all (foo, goto), or a spelling that
#      drops its apostrophe or letters.
#
# Words with a current common sense stay, even where a name shares the spelling
# (chi, java, lance), and so do informal words and interjections (admin, hmm, okay).
# The list holds the taboo forms the lexsync 0.1 demonstrations selected from en.csv
# (bitch, boobs, fuck and others) and every form meeting the criteria that the
# demonstrations selected afterwards, reviewed until a run selected none of them.
# Each exclusion moves the selections, so a change that moves them needs the same
# review. One word per line. Blank lines and lines beginning '#' are skipped.
