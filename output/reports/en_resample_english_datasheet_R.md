## Methods paragraph

40 items per condition were selected from the English lexicon (see corpora/ATTRIBUTION.md for corpus licence and citation) and matched item by item on length, n_density, old20 using lexsync's standardised_euclidean matcher. The realised control was tight: the largest standardised difference on any matched dimension was 0.04 (90% CI [-0.17, 0.26]), within the 0.5-SD equivalence bound. 3 disjoint matched item sets were drawn, so items can be treated as a random factor. Materials were counterbalanced into 1 list(s) (factorial) and generated for PsychoPy, OpenSesame and jsPsych. The selection is deterministic and reproducible (seed 2026; lexsync 0.1.0).
