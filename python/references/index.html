    References¶

    The guides and the API reference cite published work throughout, and this page carries the full entries so that every citation on the site resolves. Each citation in the guides links to the entry below, and each entry carries a DOI wherever the work has one. The R package lists the same works at the foot of each vignette that cites them. The lexical corpora that lexsync reads are attributed separately, in corpora/ATTRIBUTION.md and in the corpus registry, since their licences travel with the derived data rather than with the software.

    Armstrong, B. C., Watson, C. E., & Plaut, D. C. (2012). SOS! An algorithm and software for the stochastic optimization of stimuli. Behavior Research Methods, 44(3), 675–705. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-011-0182-9

    Austin, P. C. (2009). Balance diagnostics for comparing the distribution of baseline covariates between treatment groups in propensity-score matched samples. Statistics in Medicine, 28(25), 3083–3107. https://doi.org/10.1002/sim.3697

    Baayen, R. H., Davidson, D. J., & Bates, D. M. (2008). Mixed-effects modeling with crossed random effects for subjects and items. Journal of Memory and Language, 59(4), 390–412. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jml.2007.12.005
